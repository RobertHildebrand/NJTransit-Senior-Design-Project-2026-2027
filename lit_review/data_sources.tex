\noindent This document records where every number, file, and parameter used in the project comes from, how it was processed, and how much to trust it. Update it whenever a source is added or replaced. Links were accessed on September 28, 2026 unless noted.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Trust levels used below.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} --- official agency data or documents.
\item \textbf{B} --- reputable secondary sources, such as news reports quoting the agency, or Wikipedia citing the agency.
\item \textbf{C} --- third-party mirrors, or data that was machine-extracted.
\item \textbf{P} --- placeholder assumptions made by the team, to be replaced with client data.
\end{itemize}

\section{Data to request from NJ TRANSIT}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Hold sheets since Feb.~14, 2026: cars available and down, recorded at 6\,am and 6\,pm. This is the key input.
\item Work orders and failure logs by car: date, failure type, mileage, and days out of service.
\item Delay, cancellation, and incident logs by train, with causes.
\item The current slow-order list and the track chart: siding limits, switches, and speed limits.
\item Ridership: automatic passenger counts (if equipped), ticket machine and mobile-app sales or activations by station and hour, and any recent onboard survey. Ask DVRPC for the raw 2010 survey.
\item Minimum layover times, consist rules, crew constraints, and where protect cars can be staged.
\item The names of NJT's scheduling system, maintenance system, and dashboard platform, and access to the River LINE real-time feed.
\end{enumerate}
